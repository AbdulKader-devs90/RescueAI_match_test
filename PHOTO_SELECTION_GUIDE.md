# Profile-photo selection pattern

Derived from the 16 editor selections on 2 November 2026.

Choose a raw, individual-dog photo that:

- puts the dog in the foreground, with the head and eyes clearly visible;
- uses a front-facing or gentle three-quarter pose with direct engagement;
- is sharp, naturally lit, and works in a tall card crop;
- keeps the background outdoors and visually simple enough that the dog remains the focal point;
- shows some body context when it supports the dog's character or size.

Avoid collages, text overlays, group shots, distant dogs, obstructed faces, and images dominated by people or busy shelter surroundings. A lead/harness is acceptable when the face remains the clear focal point.
